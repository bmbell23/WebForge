Text shortcuts

Select text in a page and press Ctrl+J (or right-click it) to open a matching URL. $0 is the whole match, $1–$9 are capture groups. First matching rule wins. Matching ignores case.

Apps

Each app is one pinned tab of its own, and its whole site opens in it. Its key follows its place in the Order list at the top (Ctrl+Space then the key, or F-keys); only the first eight places have a key. Links open in is the Persona a page opened from the app lands in. Removing an app keeps its tabs.

Personas

Named workspaces with their own tab list and their own hotkey bindings. Opening a URL that matches a Persona's rules switches you to it automatically; anything unmatched lands in Unassigned. Each Persona's keys follow its place in the Order list at the top.

One rule per line. Every rule is anchored at the start and open-ended at the end, so https://host also matches https://host/deep/path.
• prefix — https://jira.company.com
• wildcard — https://*.devops.example.com matches any subdomain. * can match nothing, so https://*devops.example.com also covers the bare domain.
• regex — wrap in slashes: /^https:\/\/(ci|cd)\.example\.com/. An invalid pattern simply matches nothing rather than breaking routing.
